# Code of conduct

## Commitment

Participants keep the space respectful, technical and inclusive. Disagreement
about data, models, licenses and evaluation is welcome when it is specific and
in good faith.

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Criticize ideas, results and changes, never people.
- Declare uncertainty, conflicts of interest and limits of the evidence.
- Respect privacy, authorship and licenses.
- Accept technical review and correct mistakes when they are found.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, exposure of personal data, publishing
secrets, authorship fraud, falsifying metrics, and pressuring others to ignore
licenses or safety.

## Enforcement

Reports go privately to the maintainer. The maintainer may ask for a behavior
change, remove content, or block participation depending on severity and
context.
